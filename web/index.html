Your ROM stays with you

    
    
      
  • The whole build runs here in your browser, in Python compiled to WebAssembly. Your ROM is read from your disk into this page's memory and nowhere else: it is not uploaded, and nothing is stored.
    • 
      
  • The cartridge you save contains data converted from your ROM. It is for you, the ROM's owner, only: please don't share it.
    • 
    

  


  

    
Playing it

    
    
      
  • The image is a 512KB SuperCart. In the atari800 emulator: atari800 -5200 -cart-type 74 -cart dq52.bin.
    • 
      
  • On real hardware, use a flash cartridge or FPGA system that supports 512KB SuperCart banking.
    • 
      
  • Building it yourself instead: see the project page.
